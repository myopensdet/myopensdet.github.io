(SDET means Software Development Engineer in Test: a tester who writes code to test software.)

## Why I made this

Everything on this site comes from two places: my own work as a tester, and what many generous people have shared online: official documentation, blog posts, talks, open-source projects and answers in forums.

None of it is secret. It was just scattered. I put it in one place, in order, in plain words, and checked every example, so that anyone can learn from it: someone who has never tested, an engineer stuck on a task today, or an experienced engineer looking for a template to adapt.

Thank you to everyone whose work made this possible.

## Why this site exists

Many testers are told at work, "test the database" or "set up BDD", with no one to show them how. Good material exists, but it is spread across many places, often assumes you can already code, and rarely shows how to plan the work, or what to do when you are stuck.

myOpenSDET puts that road in one place:

- **Every page stands on its own.** If you land here from a search, the page tells you what you need to know first and links to it.
- **Real templates.** The scripts, code snippets, checklists and plans people use at work, ready to copy and adapt. Every code template is tested.
- **Learn from the page.** Every step shows its result, so you can learn without installing anything. The Practice App, a small online shop built for testing, is there if you want to practise.
- **Plain words first.** Every idea starts with an everyday example, then the real thing. Every new word is explained.
- **Official sources.** Every page links to the original documentation, so you can go deeper on your own.

## Your privacy

This site uses no cookies and no ads. Fonts and styles load from this site itself, not from other companies.
When you tick a skill or mark a lesson as done, it is saved only in your own browser. It is never sent anywhere, and clearing your browser's data for this site removes it.
The share buttons are plain links. Nothing is sent to LinkedIn, X or WhatsApp unless you click one.
If visitor counting is added later, it will be a privacy-friendly counter that stores no personal data, and this section will say so.

## Help make it better

myOpenSDET is open source, and suggestions are welcome. Here is how a change gets in:

1. **Anyone can suggest a change.** The "Edit this page" pencil at the top of a page opens a copy on GitHub. What you change there becomes a **suggestion** (a pull request), not a live edit.
2. **Nothing goes live without review.** The maintainer reads every suggestion, runs the checks, and approves it, asks for changes, or explains why not.
3. **Bigger ideas start as an issue.** For a new topic or a rewrite, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/myopensdet/myopensdet.github.io) first, so no one spends a weekend on something that won't fit.

The rules for every suggestion are short:

- Every output on a page comes from a **real run**. Never invent one.
- Write in **your own words** and link the source. Never copy text from books, courses or other sites.
- **No secrets, no real personal data, no employer or client material.**
- No ads, affiliate links or tracking.
- One fix or one topic per suggestion.

The full rules are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/myopensdet/myopensdet.github.io/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). Security problems go privately, as [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/myopensdet/myopensdet.github.io/blob/main/.github/SECURITY.md) explains, never in a public issue.
