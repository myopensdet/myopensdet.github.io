# Questions to ask in your first weeks

Ask the right person, and write the answer down so you only ask once.

## Your lead

- What does a good first month look like for me? How will we know?
- Which part of testing worries you most right now?
- What happened in the last bad release or incident?
- Who should I get to know first?

## Developers

- Which part of the system is the most fragile? Where do bugs come back?
- How do you run the app on your computer?
- What do you wish the tests checked that they don't?
- How should I give you bugs: ticket, chat, or both?

## The product owner

- Who uses this, and what do they do most?
- What would hurt the business most if it broke?
- What's coming in the next three months?

## Testers who were here before you

- Which tests do people trust, and which do they ignore?
- What's always broken in the test environment?
- What did you want to fix but never had time for?

## About the test suite

- How long does a full run take? When does it run?
- Who looks at the nightly results?
- Why is <this test> skipped? Is the ticket still open?
- Is there anything I must never run, or never run against a certain environment?
