# Pairing session: tester and developer

**Length:** 30 to 60 minutes. **Where:** one screen (shared, or side by side).
**Roles:** one types (driver), one thinks ahead and asks (navigator). Swap every 15 minutes.

## Good things to pair on

- A bug that "works on my machine": reproduce it together
- Writing the tests for a new story, before or while the code is written
- A flaky test: run it together until you see the cause
- The developer shows the code; the tester asks "what if...?"

## Before

- [ ] One clear goal: "<reproduce SHOP-433>" or "<tests for AC1 to AC3 of SHOP-420>"
- [ ] The environment works for both of you

## During

- [ ] Think out loud
- [ ] Questions are welcome; nobody grabs the keyboard
- [ ] Write down what you find as you go

## After (5 minutes)

- What did we find or finish?
- What's left, and who does it?
- What did each of us learn?
