# User story review: refund request

## Questions for the product owner

| # | Question | Answer | Story updated? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Recent orders": how many days after delivery? | 30 days after delivery | Yes |
| 2 | "Processed fast": by when must the money arrive? | Approved or refused within 2 working days. The bank may take 5 more | Yes |
| 3 | Is there an email when a refund is refused, too? | Yes, with the reason | Yes |
| 4 | "Items on sale may not be refundable": are they, or not? | Sale items can be refunded only if faulty | Yes |
| 5 | "Card, PayPal, etc": what else? Gift cards? | Card and PayPal go back to the same account. Gift cards become store credit | Yes |
| 6 | "Errors are handled properly": which errors, and what does the customer see? | The original card is expired or closed: we send store credit and email the customer | Yes |
| 7 | Missing: can a customer refund part of an order? | Yes, item by item | Yes |
| 8 | Missing: can an order be refunded twice? | No. Each item once | Yes |
| 9 | Missing: what about orders not delivered yet? | Not here: those use "cancel order" | Yes |
