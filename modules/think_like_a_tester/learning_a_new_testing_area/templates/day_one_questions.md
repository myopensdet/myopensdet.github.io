# Day-one questions: <feature or area>

Ask these before you plan or write any test. Write the answer next to each
question, with the name of the person who answered and the date.

## The ask

- [ ] What exactly should I test? What is out of scope?
- [ ] Why now? (new feature, a bug in production, an audit, a release)
- [ ] What does "done" mean for this task? Who decides?
- [ ] When is it due? Is the date fixed or flexible?

## The system

- [ ] Is there a diagram, design document or user story I can read?
- [ ] Which parts does it touch? (web page, API, database, email, other services)
- [ ] Who built it, and who can answer questions about it?
- [ ] What changed recently? Where have bugs appeared before?

## Environments and access

- [ ] Which environment do I test in? (local, test, staging)
- [ ] Which accounts and test data can I use? How do I reset them?
- [ ] Do I need access to logs, the database, or an email inbox?
- [ ] Are there tests already? Where are they, and do they pass?

## Risk

- [ ] What is the worst thing that could go wrong for a user?
- [ ] What would cost the company money, data or trust?
- [ ] Is there anything I must not do? (real payments, real emails, shared data)
